📻 Interactive Educational Radio

Designed and built an interactive traditional instrument showcase housed in a FabLab laser-cut wooden enclosure (designed via Inkscape). Powered by Arduino Uno, DFPlayer Mini, capacitive touch sensors, and LCD display.

Arduino-based educational kit using ultrasonic sensors to automate free-fall physics experiments and accurately measure gravitational acceleration (g) for secondary students.
